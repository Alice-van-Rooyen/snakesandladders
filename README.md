“This board isn’t meant to be efficient or strategic.
It’s meant to feel like recovery.

You can only move one step at a time, and you can’t see very far ahead. Sometimes you’re pulled backwards. Sometimes things that looked clear become confusing again.

That’s deliberate. Recovery often isn’t a straight line, and people living inside it don’t experience progress as neatly as clinical timelines suggest.

The goal here isn’t to ‘win’ — it’s to experience what uncertainty, fatigue, and interrupted momentum feel like from the inside.

That experience is what this board is trying to make visible.”
